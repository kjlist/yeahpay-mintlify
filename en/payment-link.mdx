Payment Link lets you collect payment through a shareable link or QR code. Create and manage links in the desktop merchant dashboard or mobile app. Buyers open the link or scan the code and choose how to pay.

## Product features

- **Set an amount or add products.** Enter a fixed amount or build an order with multiple products. Available currencies depend on your merchant account.
- **Customize the page and payment rules.** Set your logo and brand colors, choose which buyer details to collect, and set an expiry date or payment limit.
- **Share and manage links across channels.** Send the link by chat, email, or text, or display a QR code in person. Review the same payment records on desktop or in the app. You can also enable payment confirmation emails.

## Start collecting payments

1. Ask your account manager to enable Payment Link.
2. Create a link in the merchant dashboard or app. Add an amount or products and set the payment rules.
3. Share the link or QR code. Check payment records in the merchant dashboard or app.
