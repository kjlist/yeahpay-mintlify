## Product overview

Payment Link is a no-code way to collect payments with YeahPay. In a few steps on the desktop merchant dashboard or mobile app, create a payment link or QR code to share by chat, email, text, social media, or in person. Buyers can open the link and pay immediately. The dashboard and app sync in real time and use the same field rules. Payment Link supports multiple currencies, custom branding, and real-time notifications for successful payments. It suits use cases such as social commerce, in-person sales, phone bookings, and membership fees.

## Key features and benefits

- **No-code setup**. Merchants of any size can create payment links in a few steps from the desktop dashboard or mobile app.
- **Desktop and mobile management**. Both platforms use the same fields and rules, so you can create and manage links from either one.
- **Flexible sharing**. Copy a link, generate a QR code, or send it by email for use cases such as social commerce and in-person sales.
- **Custom branding**. Set your logo and brand color. Checkout and payment link pages apply them automatically to help build buyer trust.
- **Multiple currencies**. Collect payments in currencies such as HKD, SGD, and USD based on the currencies enabled for your merchant account.
- **Configurable links**. Set a fixed amount or build a multi-product order, collect addresses, phone numbers, or buyer notes, and customize the greeting.
- **Real-time notifications**. After each successful payment, YeahPay automatically notifies the configured merchant email addresses. You can configure more than one.
- **Security and controls**. Set link expiry and use limits. Editable fields depend on the link status to help prevent duplicate collections or misuse.

## Getting started

1. **Enable Payment Link**. Contact your account manager to enable the feature. Once enabled, it appears in the desktop dashboard and mobile app.
2. **Create a payment link**. Enter the basic details and set a fixed amount or add multiple products. Configure buyer information collection, use limits, and link expiry as needed, then generate the link.
3. **Share the link with buyers**. Copy the link, generate a QR code, or use the built-in email template. Share it by chat, social media, text, or in person.
4. **The buyer pays**. The buyer opens the link, chooses a payment method on the desktop- or mobile-friendly payment page, and pays.
5. **Receive a payment notification**. If notifications are enabled, YeahPay automatically sends a notice to the configured merchant email addresses after each successful payment.
6. **View and manage orders**. Use the payment link list or detail view in the desktop dashboard or mobile app to review transactions, edit links, or disable them, depending on their status.

## Frequently asked questions

**What if I cannot find Payment Link in my account?** The YeahPay operations team controls access. Contact your account manager to request activation.

**Can I share one payment link with multiple buyers?** Yes. If you set a use limit, the link automatically closes and shows as completed once that limit is reached. With no use limit, buyers can use it as often as needed before it expires.

**Why can't I delete a payment link?** Disable the link to stop accepting payments. Disabled links remain in the list so you can review historical records.

**Can I use the same links on desktop and mobile?** Yes. The dashboard and app sync in real time. Links you create or edit on either platform appear immediately on the other.

**What if an email notification fails to send?** Wait 60 seconds and try again, or check the recipient's spam folder. If the problem persists, share the link or QR code directly or contact technical support.
