## Choose a payment product

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Unified Checkout" icon="credit-card" href="/en/unified-checkout">
    Send website or app buyers to a YeahPay hosted page to pay by card or local wallet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Embedded Checkout" icon="code" href="/en/embedded-checkout">
    Offer card payments and let buyers save or reuse a card on your own checkout page.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payment Link" icon="link" href="/en/payment-link">
    Collect payment by link or QR code for chat sales, phone bookings, or events.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AgentPay" icon="robot" href="/en/agentpay">
    Accept payments from AI agents or let an enterprise agent make authorized purchases.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Subscriptions and renewals

For memberships, software, and content, use [Subscription Payments](/en/subscription-payment) to collect the first payment and charge on your own billing schedule once the buyer has granted consent.
