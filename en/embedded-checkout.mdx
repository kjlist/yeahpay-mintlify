Embedded Checkout places a card payment component on your own checkout page. Buyers can enter or select a card and complete 3D Secure verification when required, within your checkout flow.

## Product features

- **Payment on your own page.** Choose where the checkout component appears. The YeahPay component collects and processes card numbers, expiry dates, and security codes.
- **Save and reuse cards.** Buyers can save a card during payment or add one separately, then select a saved card on a future purchase.
- **Immediate charges and preauthorization.** Charge at order confirmation, or reserve an amount and capture it after providing the service.

## Use cases

An online store can keep payment in its existing checkout and offer saved cards to repeat buyers. Hotels and rental businesses can use preauthorization to reserve funds before providing a service.
