Subscription Payments let you collect recurring payments for memberships, software, or content. Buyers make their first payment and grant consent in Unified Checkout. You can then initiate subsequent charges under that consent.

## How it works

You manage plans, billing dates, and renewal rules. YeahPay handles the initial payment, consent management, and subsequent charges. Buyers can renew under active consent without returning to checkout.
