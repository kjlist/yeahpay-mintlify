Unified Checkout brings multiple payment methods to your website or app through a YeahPay hosted page. Buyers review their order, select a payment method, and pay on that page. They can then return to your site or app.

## Product features

- **Multiple payment methods.** Accept cards and local wallets, including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, WeChat Pay, and Alipay. Octopus is available on mobile in Hong Kong. Buyers see the methods available in their region and enabled for your account.
- **Desktop and mobile checkout.** Open the same checkout from a desktop browser, mobile browser, or app. The checkout is available in Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, and Japanese.
- **Card details handled by YeahPay.** YeahPay collects and processes the card number and security code on its hosted page.

## Use cases

An online store can send buyers to a hosted page to pay for an order. Merchants selling in multiple markets can offer cards alongside the wallets their customers use locally.
